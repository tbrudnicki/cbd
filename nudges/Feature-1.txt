Introduce a lightweight, non-intrusive Net Promoter Score (NPS) survey within the Up by CBD app to capture customer feedback after selected successful journeys and at configured periodic intervals.
Customers who provide a score of 9–10 will be classified as promoters and invited to rate Up by CBD on the relevant app store. Customers who provide a score of 0–8 will be asked to provide additional feedback, which will be recorded internally for analysis and service improvement.
Business Objective
Measure customer satisfaction at relevant moments.
Capture journey-specific feedback.
Identify product and service improvement opportunities.
Encourage promoters to provide public app-store ratings.
Establish a configurable framework for future survey triggers.
NPS Triggers
Display the survey after successful completion of:
Debit card activation
Card limit increase, after the consent screen
First International Transfer
Third Local Transfer
WPS enrolment
Invoice creation
Periodically, every three months
The survey must not appear if the underlying journey is unsuccessful, pending, or interrupted.
Customer Journey
Step 1: NPS Rating
Display the question:
How likely are you to recommend Up by CBD to a friend or colleague?
Provide:
Rating scale from 0 to 10
Skip or close option
Clear rating selection state
Continue or Submit CTA
Step 2: Response-Based Journey
Score 9–10: Promoter
Save the rating internally.
Display a positive acknowledgement.
Invite the customer to rate Up by CBD.
Redirect to the Apple App Store on iOS or Google Play Store on Android.
Provide a Maybe Later option.
Suggested message:
Thank you for your feedback! We’d love for you to share your experience on the App Store.
Score 0–8: Passive or Detractor
Save the rating internally.
Display a feedback form.
Allow selection of one or more predefined reasons.
Provide an optional free-text comments field.
Do not redirect the customer to an app store.
Suggested reasons:
App performance
Payments experience
Account servicing
Navigation
Feature functionality
Other
Confirmation message:
Your feedback has been recorded and will help us improve your experience.
Periodic NPS Rules
Display the periodic NPS survey every three months.
Allow the customer to skip or dismiss the survey up to three times.
Record each skip against the periodic survey cycle.
After the third skip, suppress the periodic survey for three months.
Reset the skip count when a new eligible periodic survey cycle begins.
Transaction-triggered and periodic survey rules must prevent overlapping or repeated prompts within a configurable suppression period.
Reporting Requirements
A Power BI dashboard shall be created to monitor NPS performance and customer feedback for a rolling 60-day period.
The dashboard shall include:
Total surveys displayed, submitted, and skipped
Survey response and completion rates
NPS score and rating distribution from 0 to 10
Promoter, passive, and detractor volumes and percentages
Results by trigger journey, customer segment, device platform, and date
Feedback reasons and comments for scores from 0 to 8
App-store prompt and redirect volumes for scores of 9 and 10
Submission, network, and app-store redirection failures
Periodic-survey skip counts and suppression volumes
Daily and weekly NPS trends
Raw Data Export
Power BI shall allow authorized users to export the underlying raw data for the selected reporting period.
The default reporting period shall be the latest 60 days.
Users shall be able to apply filters before exporting the data.
The export shall include rating, feedback reason, comments, trigger journey, customer segment, device platform, event timestamps, app-store actions, skip count, and submission status.
Customer identifiers shall be masked or access-controlled in line with the bank’s data-security and privacy requirements.
Dashboard access, data export, and personally identifiable information visibility shall be controlled through role-based access.

nps_survey_displayed
nps_survey_skipped
nps_survey_dismissed
nps_rating_selected
nps_feedback_submitted

nps_app_store_redirect_clicked
nps_app_store_redirect_failed
nps_maybe_later_selected
nps_periodic_survey_suppressed
Common Event Attributes
Each applicable event shall include:
Customer ID or approved masked identifier
Company ID
Survey ID and survey version
Trigger journey
Trigger type: journey-based or periodic
Rating selected
Customer classification: promoter, passive, or detractor
Feedback reasons selected
Free-text comment indicator
Skip count
Suppression status and expiry date
Device platform and operating-system version
Application version
Event timestamp
Submission or failure status
Error code, where applicable
Feedback comments containing customer-entered text should only be sent to MoEngage where approved by Information Security, Compliance, and Data Privacy; otherwise, only the comment-submitted indicator and internal feedback reference should be captured.


Key Configuration Requirements
The backend or middleware should support configuration of:
Eligible journeys and trigger events
First or nth transaction rules
Periodic survey frequency
Skip limit and suppression period
Promoter threshold
Feedback reasons
Free-text field availability and character limit
Survey and confirmation copy
Apple App Store and Google Play Store URLs
Journey-level cooling-off period
Feature-level enablement or disablement
Customer and segment eligibility

High-Level Acceptance Criteria
AC1: Journey-Based Trigger
The NPS survey shall be displayed only after successful completion of a configured eligible journey.
AC2: Periodic Trigger
The periodic NPS survey shall be presented every three months, subject to configured eligibility, skip, and suppression rules.
AC3: Rating Capture
The customer shall be able to select and submit one score from 0 to 10, with the selected rating clearly highlighted.
AC4: Promoter Journey
A customer who submits a score of 9 or 10 shall receive an app-store review prompt with Rate the App and Maybe Later options.
AC5: App-Store Redirection
Selecting Rate the App shall open the Apple App Store on iOS or Google Play Store on Android using the configured store URL.
AC6: Feedback Journey
A customer who submits a score from 0 to 8 shall be shown configurable feedback reasons and an optional comments field without being redirected to an app store.
AC7: Feedback Storage
The submitted rating, feedback reason, comments, journey, customer identifier, device platform, and submission timestamp shall be recorded internally.
AC8: Skip Handling
Each periodic survey dismissal shall increment the skip count, and the survey shall be suppressed for three months after the third skip.
AC9: Submission Confirmation
A confirmation message shall be displayed after the customer’s feedback is successfully recorded.
AC10: Error Handling
If feedback submission fails, the app shall display “We couldn’t submit your feedback right now. Please try again later.” with Retry and Close options.
AC11: Redirection Failure
If the app store cannot be opened, the app shall display “Unable to open the App Store at the moment.”
AC12: Configuration
Survey triggers, frequency, rating thresholds, suppression periods, skip limits, feedback reasons, content, and app-store URLs shall be backend configurable without an app release.
AC13: Power BI shall provide NPS reporting for a rolling 60-day period with filters for date, trigger journey, rating category, customer segment, and device platform.
AC14: Authorized Power BI users shall be able to export the filtered underlying raw data, subject to role-based access and data-masking controls.
AC15: Relevant NPS interaction, submission, skip, redirect, suppression, and failure events shall be captured in MoEngage with the defined event attributes.
AC16: Power BI data and MoEngage events shall reconcile with the source NPS submissions using a common survey or feedback reference.
AC17: Dashboard refresh frequency, reporting dimensions, event names, event attributes, and data-retention rules shall be configurable without requiring an app release.
